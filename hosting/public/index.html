Privacy Policy

Expense Tracker — Last updated: September 10, 2026

1. Introduction

Expense Tracker ("we", "our", or "the app") is a personal finance tracking application. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal data. This privacy policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information.

2. Information We Collect

We collect the following types of information:

  • Account Information: When you sign in with Google, we receive your name, email address, and profile picture from Google Sign-In.
  • Financial Data: Expense and income entries you create (amounts, categories, descriptions, dates). This data is stored locally on your device.
  • Notification Data: If you enable the UPI auto-detect feature, the app reads payment notifications from UPI apps (like Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm) to automatically log transactions. We only extract transaction amounts and merchant names — no other notification content is read or stored.

3. How We Use Your Information

  • To provide and maintain the expense tracking functionality
  • To authenticate your identity via Google Sign-In
  • To automatically detect and log UPI transactions (only when you grant notification access)
  • To display charts, analytics, and financial summaries

4. Data Storage & Security

Your expense data is stored locally on your device using a SQLite database. We do not upload your financial data to any external server. Authentication is handled securely through Firebase Authentication and Google Sign-In.

5. Notification Access

The app requests Notification Listener permission solely to detect UPI payment notifications for automatic expense logging. This permission is optional — the app works fully without it. When enabled:

  • We only process notifications from known UPI payment apps
  • We extract only transaction amount and merchant name
  • No notification data is transmitted to external servers
  • You can revoke this permission at any time from device settings

6. Third-Party Services

The app uses the following third-party services:

  • Firebase Authentication: For secure user sign-in (Firebase Privacy Policy)
  • Google Sign-In: For account authentication (Google Privacy Policy)

7. Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or share your personal data with any third parties. Your financial data never leaves your device.

8. Children's Privacy

This app is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13.

9. Your Rights

You can:

  • Delete all your data by uninstalling the app (data is stored locally)
  • Revoke notification access from device settings at any time
  • Sign out and remove your Google account connection

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Changes will be reflected on this page with an updated date.
